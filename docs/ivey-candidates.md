The [PHH repository](https://github.com/uoftcprg/phh-dataset) also contains large
anonymized and bot datasets. Anonymized players cannot be identified as Ivey
from style or a convenient alias. Pluribus or ACPC agreement would be a separate
benchmark and must never be reported as Ivey agreement.

A collection of highlighted hands still cannot establish representative
accuracy across all of Ivey's decisions. Retain this exclusion record instead
of padding the sample count with ambiguous reports.
